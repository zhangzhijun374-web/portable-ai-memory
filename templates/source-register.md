## Review status definitions

- `full`: full source reviewed.
- `partial`: only selected pages, sections, or records reviewed.
- `summary_only`: only a summary or prior conversation note was available.
- `not_reviewed`: source identified but not inspected.

Do not describe a source as fully reviewed if only an excerpt or summary was available.
